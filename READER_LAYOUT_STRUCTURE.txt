READER LAYOUT STRUCTURE

config/reader-config.js
  Reader shell settings: title, book URL, font steps and TOC selectors.

config/layout-rules.js
  Book layout settings used inside the iframe:
  - pagination geometry;
  - image composition rules;
  - static image profiles for 85%, 100% and 125%.

app/book-bridge.js
  Pagination and composition algorithms. It reads values from layout-rules.js.

To replace the InDesign HTML, keep its paragraph/object class names and ensure
config/layout-rules.js is loaded immediately before app/book-bridge.js.
